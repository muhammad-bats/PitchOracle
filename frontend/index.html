⚙️ Hybrid Ensemble Architecture & Methodology

How PitchOracle combines gradient boosted trees with statistical count models

1. XGBoost Multi-Class Classifier (40% Weight)

Gradient boosted decision trees (`max_depth=4, n_estimators=220, learning_rate=0.045`) trained on 28 strictly pre-match features using `multi:softprob` objective. Captures non-linear interactions between Club Elo momentum, venue-specific scoring rates, and H2H dominance.

2. Random Forest Classifier (25% Weight)

Bagged ensemble of 260 deep decision trees (`min_samples_leaf=8`) providing variance reduction and calibrated out-of-bag probability estimates in high-parity matchups.

3. Dixon-Coles Bivariate Poisson + 10,000x Monte Carlo (35% Weight)

Estimates club attacking ($\alpha_i$) and defensive ($\beta_j$) rates with low-score correlation parameter $\rho = -0.085$ to accurately model `0-0`, `1-0`, `0-1`, and `1-1` scorelines, generating the 7×7 exact score matrix and 90-minute match telemetry.
